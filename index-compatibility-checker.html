Index pairs to check

Paste a two-column i7/i5 list, or three columns: index name, i7, i5. Excel, CSV, and pairs such as ACGTACGT+TGCATGCA all work.

Pair distance is i7 distance + i5 distance. Exact pair collision means both distances are zero.

How the checker interprets your list
  • Blank lines and a header containing “i7” and “i5” are ignored.
  • Tabs, commas, semicolons, or spaces separate columns. With three or more columns, the final two are read as i7 and i5; earlier columns form the index name.
  • One-column pairs joined with +, /, :, or | are accepted.
  • Indexes must contain A, C, G, or T only, and all compared indexes must have equal lengths.
  • For the combined-pair rule, the distance is distance(i7) + distance(i5), equivalent to taking the Hamming distance after concatenating i7 and i5.
